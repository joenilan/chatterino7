## Product and workflow requirements

- Full Chatterino7 feature recreation with Jawjack's own modern design. Keep the
  original C++ implementation as a behavior/reference source, not the runtime UI.
- Dark Studio design, custom titlebar, GPUI Kit, restrained pastel indicators,
  polished hover/focus/loading/error states and smooth motion with reduced-motion
  support. Compact windows must remain usable; do not force a small startup size.
- Hamburger, account and settings belong in the titlebar. Workspace navigation
  belongs in the collapsible sidebar; do not add a duplicate workspace tab strip.
- Channel tabs, plus-to-add, right-click menus, middle-click close with confirmation,
  recursive splits limited by screen space, drag-to-split/recombine, and persisted
  layout. One composer per visible chat group, with channel/account-safe drafts.
- Terminal-style text selection and right-click copy, selection-clear and feedback.
  Ordinary blank transcript areas must not show irrelevant Ctrl-click tooltips.
- Emote browser uses scrolling service/streamer-avatar collections, not pagination.
  Subscription emotes load after login; no separate enable-subscription button.
  Keep the working bounded image cache, visible-first loading, fades, retry and
  virtualization. Hover cards show larger animated media and source information.
- Unread counters belong on inactive chats or when reading above the live edge;
  avoid transient counter flashes while already following the bottom.
- Make meaningful batches, not one small cosmetic change followed by a rebuild
  request. Keep source implementation, build evidence and native acceptance distinct.
- Do not add fixture/test buttons or a test dashboard to the application. Prefer
  driving the real window through its existing control interface and screenshots.
- Do not start GitHub Actions/CI or create PRs for this work. Use `[skip ci]` on
  commits. Follow the owner's current publication authorization and preserve
  unrelated changes; never force-push over someone else's work.
- Use targeted checks for actual new logic/bugs rather than repeatedly running
  broad suites. A successful build alone does not establish visual or runtime correctness.

Important implementation limits:

- 7TV paints support a bounded subset of static gradients. Image, shadow, layered
  and other unsupported effects fall back. Do not advertise full cosmetic parity.
- Explicit local username colors take precedence over paints. Glyph geometry,
  wrapping, copy and selection must remain unchanged by paint rendering.
- Recent emotes are session-only and recorded on successful matching sends, not
  picker clicks or failed sends. Availability is revalidated against current catalogs.
- Notes are plain-text local profile data, not encrypted; they are not sent to
  providers or inserted into ordinary chat transcripts/cards automatically.
- FFZ room cosmetics require matching channel identity and are suppressed for
  Shared Chat source messages. Global grants follow sender IDs. Cosmetics never
  grant moderator permissions. Complete snapshots replace prior assignments.
- FFZ supporter loading uses a separate anonymous bounded worker, 15-minute
  success refresh, 60-second failure retry and 30-minute last-good expiry. Room
  grants also expire. Status-only failures do not trigger full transcript re-expansion.

## Approved future design to preserve

Viewer, Moderator and Streamer are presentation arrangements, not permission grants.
The full design is in ROLE_EXPERIENCES.md and is not implemented merely because
the document exists. Avoid adding a shallow mode toggle that promises working tools.

Streamer mode must maintain an account-level own-chat anchor as the first tab in
the primary group of **every workspace**, independent of workspace ownership.
It cannot be moved, closed or hidden by live-only filtering while enabled. The
feed, own-channel draft/reply target and Studio session must survive workspace
switches without duplicate subscriptions or resets. Account changes isolate
drafts and rebind only after verified identity. Returning to Viewer removes the
protection without deleting the channel or layout.

Future Connections/Integrations should support the owner's and other people's
streams on additional platforms where official access permits it. YouTube and
Kick are more important than TikTok. This extends full Chatterino parity; it does
not replace or reduce it. Keep the UI chat-first and avoid permanent tool panels
that consume compact-window space.
